Photo Booth Hire Christchurch

Instant fun, and something to take home.

A photo booth brings energy and personalised moments to any event. It suits weddings, corporate functions, brand activations, formals, parties, and festivals.

There are options for digital sharing and custom branded printouts. Booths are available across New Zealand.

Black Booth

Mirror Booth

FAQ

What comes with a photo booth?

The Minimal, Black, and Mirror booths come with unlimited printouts, an operator, a customised backdrop and photo strip design, a box of props, and digital copies after the event.

Which photo booth is the most popular?

The Mirror Booth is our most popular booth.

Can I hire a booth without an operator?

Yes. Our iPad booth is a paperless dry hire option with no operator needed.

How many people fit in the 360 booth?

The 360 video booth fits up to 4 people comfortably.
