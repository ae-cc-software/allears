AV Hire and Event Production Services in Christchurch

AV production and equipment hire, based in Christchurch, working nationwide.

We supply event infrastructure and live production. Small functions through to multi-day music festivals.

Based in Christchurch and working across the country, All Ears delivers full-scale event production including marquees, staging, lighting, sound, power, and more. We support all events from weddings to festivals using reliable high quality equipment. No drama. No surprises.
