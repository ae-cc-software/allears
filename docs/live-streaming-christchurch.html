Live Streaming and Event Recording Christchurch

Share your event with the people who can't be in the room.

We deliver live streams for corporate events, hybrid conferences, weddings, and funerals. Whether you are connecting remote speakers or sharing a special moment with loved ones online, each stream is set up to suit the event.

Our team works with gear from Blackmagic Design, Sony, and Sennheiser so the broadcast stays high quality and reliable. Available across New Zealand.

How We Can Help

01.

Live Streaming

Live streams for weddings, funerals, conferences, performances, and special occasions, live on the day or captured for a scheduled broadcast after the event.

  • High quality streams
  • Scheduled broadcasts
  • Remote speakers
02.

Event Recording

Clear video and audio of presentations, theatre performances, conferences, and live shows, so you can keep, repurpose, or share it afterwards.

  • Corporate presentations
  • Theatre and live shows
  • Weddings

Screens and Lighting

  • LED screens
  • Constant lighting
  • Backdrops
