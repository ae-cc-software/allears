School Ball Production Christchurch

Plan your ball

School ball production in Christchurch and anywhere in New Zealand. Take the full package or pick a few extras: DJs, photo booths, lighting, theming and decor.

We've done balls at Rochester, Rutherford Hall, Tupuanuku and plenty of other Christchurch venues. We know the power setups, the load-in access and where to rig truss without blocking sightlines. Our crew arrives in the afternoon, builds the show, runs it all night and packs it out afterwards.

FAQ

How far in advance should we book?

As early as you can. We can only take on a limited number each year. Schools that book in Term 1 for a Term 2 or 3 ball have the best chance of getting their preferred date.

What's included in a school ball package?

A typical package includes a DJ with sound system and dance floor lighting. Add photo booths, glambots, LED screens, theming and more, and we'll quote to your vision and budget.

Can we choose our own DJ?

Yes. We'll provide the DJ gear (CDJ-3000s, mixer, booth monitor). If you don't have a DJ lined up, we can sort one.

Do you do theming?

Yes. Take a ready-to-go package (Hollywood, Starry Night, Winter Wonderland, Enchanted Forest and more) or mix and match individual items.

Can you do afterparties too?

Yes. Inflatable party cubes, marquees, silent disco headsets, and portable sound and lighting. Same crew, same night.

Do you work outside Christchurch?

Yes. We're based in Christchurch and available nationwide across New Zealand. We've done formals in venues all over Canterbury and beyond.
