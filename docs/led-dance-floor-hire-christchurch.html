LED Dance Floor Hire Christchurch

A floor that lights up under your feet.

Guests love a dance floor that reacts to them. Ours is sound-activated, so it syncs with the music and changes as the night goes on.

It comes in 1m x 1m panels, so it fits most spaces. It suits birthdays, balls, formals, weddings, fashion shows, and theatre performances, and it is available across New Zealand.

Disco LED Floor

Modern LED Floor

Yes. It is sound-activated, so the visuals sync with the music.
