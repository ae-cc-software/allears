Power Distribution Hire Christchurch

Easily overlooked, and the first thing noticed if it's missing.

Power is one of those things that gets forgotten when planning an event. We have kilometres of 3-phase cabling, dozens of distro boxes, and generators of all sizes.

Just as importantly, we have a knowledgeable team to design a solution and make it work.

What We Can Do

01.

Stage Power

Plan and set up power for stages, sound, lighting, and screens at large events.

  • 3-phase cabling
  • Distro boxes
  • Stage and production power
02.

Site Power

Get power to food trucks and other vendors across the site, from temporary food courts to A&P shows.

  • Food trucks and vendors
  • Temporary food courts
  • A&P shows
03.

Generators

A wide range of generators to get power to where it is needed, including sites with no mains supply.

  • Generators of all sizes
  • Remote sites
  • Designed for your site
