Toolshed Ball

Full production for a big night out

The Toolshed Ball needed the full treatment. Lighting, audio, backline. The venue got a complete makeover. We set up a rig that turned a plain room into a proper party. Moody lighting for the early arrivals, then building through the night to full dancefloor mode. The PA was clean and punchy, the DJ backline was set and ready, and the whole show ran smooth from first song to last. Ball production is a formula and we've got it down.
