Rhythm & Vines Backline

Decks, mixers and monitors across the festival

DJ backline at a multi-stage festival is a logistics job as much as anything. Every stage needs its own setup. CDJ-3000s, DJM-900NXS2 mixers, booth monitors, cabling. All of it has to be tested, labelled and ready before the first act walks on. We supplied and managed the DJ backline across R&V, making sure each stage had what it needed and that gear swaps happened fast when they needed to. No drama, no missing cables, no dead channels.
