TV Hire Christchurch

A TV on a stand, wherever your event needs one.

A TV is the simple way to get your content in front of a room. It stands on its own with no wall mounting or rigging, so it goes where you need it: beside a stage, behind a front desk, or in the middle of an exhibition.

We hire TVs across Christchurch and Canterbury for conferences, fundraisers, exhibitions, and school balls. Plug in a laptop or a media player and you're away.

What People Use Them For

01.

Presentations and Slides

Slides for a conference or a meeting, photos at a school ball, or the winners coming up on an awards night. Your laptop goes in and the whole room can see it.

  • Conferences and meetings
  • Awards nights
  • School balls
02.

Front Desks

Stand a TV behind the desk and people can see who you are and what's on before they reach the front of the queue. A branded loop does the talking while your team looks after people.

  • Registration desks
  • Sales and merch tables
  • Brand and campaign loops
03.

Signage and Exhibitions

Sponsor logos, menus, and auction lots on a loop, or video running alongside the work at a gallery opening. Set it going once and leave it to run for the whole event.

  • Sponsor logos and menus
  • Auction lots
  • Galleries and exhibitions

FAQ

How much does TV hire cost?

A 65" TV on a stand is $250+GST, with the stand included. Delivery and setup are extra. For other sizes, get in touch for pricing.

What size TV do I need?

For slides in a function room, a 65" is the usual pick. For a registration desk or a small meeting room you can go smaller, and if people are watching from the back of a conference room you can go bigger or talk to us about an LED screen. We also have 43" and 86" TVs, so get in touch and we will sort the right size.

Do the TVs come with stands?

Yes. The stand is included in the hire price, so there is nothing extra to hire to get it standing up.

Can you deliver and set up?

Yes, delivery and setup are extra. Pick up from our warehouse in Sydenham, or we will deliver the TV, set it up, test your content on it, and collect it after the event.
