Pipe and Drape Hire Christchurch

A quick way to make any room look finished.

Pipe and drape blocks off part of a room or simply makes a space look better, and it goes up fast. Our premium black velvet drape hides what you don't want seen and gives your event a clean, dark backdrop.

We hire it across Christchurch and Canterbury for functions, trade stands, stages, and photo backdrops. Each drape piece is 3.75m x 3.75m.

What People Use It For

01.

Dividing a Room

Close off part of a venue so the space fits the crowd, or hide the storage and service areas your guests don't need to see.

  • Function rooms
  • Back of house
  • Venue walls
02.

Stages and Backdrops

Drape behind a stage or a set of risers tidies up the view and makes the lighting stand out.

  • Stage backdrops
  • Speech and awards stages
  • Band risers
03.

Trade Stands

Define a booth, build a backdrop, and give your trade show display a polished, professional look.

  • Booth walls
  • Exhibitions
  • Brand activations

Pair it with a stage.

Drape usually goes out with a stage. We have modular staging for indoor shows and a covered stage trailer for outdoors.
